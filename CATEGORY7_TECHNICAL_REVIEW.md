# Tennessee Category 7 Technical Review

## Scope and outcome

Reviewed all 53 automated flags, representing 46 distinct questions. For every flagged question, the stem, all four options, the zero-based answer index, explanation, and internal source metadata were inspected. No question-bank content or answer index was changed.

The automated flags are review prompts, not determinations of error. The review found explanation/wording corrections for 16 items; seven additional items remain unverified because their claims are product-, site-, or Tennessee-specific and could not be confirmed from an accessible authoritative source. Other flagged items were verified, including their stated arithmetic where relevant.

### Confirmed explanation or wording defects

- **C7-201, C7-213, C7-231, C7-237, C7-242, C7-243, C7-246, and C7-247:** explanations are incomplete, malformed, or discuss a different topic than the question.
- **C7-206 and C7-235:** the restricted-use pesticide explanations do not clearly state the applicable federal/state certification and supervision requirements. C7-206's explanation also conflicts with its keyed option permitting authorized supervised personnel.
- **C7-223:** clarify that an REI is a label-specific interval in covered agricultural Worker Protection Standard contexts, not a universal reentry period for every pesticide use or workplace.
- **C7-142:** “require ... treatment” overstates what follows from finding an expansion joint; inspection does not itself establish that treatment is necessary or label-permitted.
- **C7-250:** the “every day for 70 years” risk-cup analogy is an oversimplification of EPA's tolerance safety determination.
- **C7-253:** the explanation overgeneralizes the ESA. Applicators should follow product-label requirements and any applicable, geographically specific EPA bulletins; the ESA does not categorically prohibit every action that could affect habitat.
- **C7-254:** the two-year period needs its Tennessee recordkeeping scope stated; it should not be presented as a universal retention period for every pesticide record.
- **C7-285:** “remains active” risks conflating environmental persistence with continued pesticidal activity; define persistence as how long the pesticide remains in the environment.
